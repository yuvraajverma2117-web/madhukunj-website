BEFORE YOU TAKE A BITE

A few little
good-to-knows.

How do I place an order?+

Add your favourites to the bag, fill in your details and prepare your enquiry. Review it, then open WhatsApp and send it yourself. Our team confirms availability, timing and the final amount.

Do you deliver in Bajna?+

Our printed menu lists delivery in Bajna, with free delivery above ₹200. Please confirm the current delivery area and any charge with the kitchen before ordering.

How early should I order a cake?+

The cake menu asks for at least two hours’ notice. Custom designs and larger cakes may need more time. The cake team will confirm your requested date and preparation requirements.

What about allergies and dietary requests?+

Please tell the team about any allergies before ordering. Ingredient details and cross-contact cannot be guaranteed by this website. Eggless preparation is a request for the baker to confirm.
